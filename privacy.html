Privacy Policy

Last updated · January 2026

KeyGuard Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any password you generate or analyze. Every calculation happens locally, inside your own browser.

How the tool works

KeyGuard Pro's password generator and strength checker run entirely on your device using your browser's built-in Web Crypto API. When you generate a password or type one in to check its strength, that text never leaves your browser — it is not sent to our servers, because we don't have servers that process it. We have no user accounts, no password logs, and no way to see what you've typed.

What we do not do

  • We do not store the passwords you generate or analyze.
  • We do not transmit password data to any server, ours or anyone else's.
  • We do not require an account, login, or any personal information to use the tool.
  • We do not track what passwords you create across visits.

Information we do collect

Like most websites, we use standard analytics and advertising tools to understand traffic and support the site. These tools may collect general, non-password information such as:

  • Pages visited and general usage patterns (via Google Analytics / Tag Manager)
  • Browser type, device type, and approximate location (via IP address)
  • Cookies used for advertising personalization (via Google AdSense)

None of this data includes anything you type into the password generator or strength checker fields.

Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites on the web. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics and advertising purposes as described above. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies will not affect the core functionality of the password generator or strength checker, since those tools do not rely on cookies to work.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how KeyGuard Pro works, reach out through the contact form on the main site. We read every message.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.
