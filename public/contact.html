Contact

KeyGuard Pro is run by one person, so email is the fastest way to reach it. There is no contact form on this page: a form would collect your name, address and message on a server, and the site does not need to store any of that to answer a question.

Email: keyguard-pro@outlook.com

How quickly you can expect a reply

We aim to reply within two business days. Messages that report a broken tool, a privacy question or an error in a guide are read first. The site is maintained by one person alongside other work, so a complicated question can take a little longer, and messages sent at the weekend are usually answered at the start of the following week.

What to include

  • Reporting a bug: tell us the page you were on, the browser and device you used, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. A screenshot is welcome if the problem is visual.
  • Correcting a guide: send the page address and the sentence or claim you believe is wrong, with a source if you have one. Corrections are made on the page and the revision date is updated.
  • Privacy or advertising: put "privacy" or "advertising" in the subject line. If an ad looks broken, offensive or misleading, describe the page and, if possible, the advertiser, so it can be reported to Google.
  • General questions: say which tool or guide you are asking about. It is hard to give a useful answer without that context.

Please do not send a password

We never need a real password to help you, and we cannot recover one. Passwords are generated and checked inside your browser and are not stored by KeyGuard Pro, so there is nothing on our side to retrieve. If you want to show that the strength checker is behaving oddly, use a made-up example that you do not use anywhere.

Requests about your data

The site has no accounts and keeps no database of visitors, so there is normally no personal record to delete. If you want to know how to ask for deletion, or how advertising and analytics data is handled by Google, see the Privacy Policy, which explains the request process and the settings Google provides. The Cookie Policy covers how to control cookies in your browser.

What we cannot help with

We cannot get you back into an account, reset a password on someone else's service, or tell you whether a specific account of yours has been breached beyond what the breach check and the service's own notices can show. For account recovery, contact the company that runs the account. For advice on what to do after a leak, the guide on what to do after a data breach sets out the order that matters.
