Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use keyguard-pro.xyz ("KeyGuard Pro", "we", "us"). It covers the tools, the guides and the advertising and analytics services loaded by the site.

Passwords you generate or type

Passwords are generated and analysed by JavaScript running in your browser, using your browser's cryptographic random number generator. The passwords you generate, and the text you type into the strength checker, are not sent to our servers and are not stored by us. We have no servers that process them and no user accounts, so we cannot recover a password for you.

The list of recently generated passwords that appears after you copy one exists only in the memory of the open tab. It is not saved to cookies, local storage or session storage, and it is lost when you reload or close the page. When you press a copy button, the password is placed on your device's clipboard, where other applications may read it until it is overwritten; clear the clipboard when you are finished.

The breach check page

The optional breach check page is the one place where something derived from a password leaves your browser. When you use it, your browser computes a SHA-1 hash on your device and sends only the first five characters of that hash to the Pwned Passwords service operated by Have I Been Pwned. The request asks for padded results so their size does not reveal the answer. The service returns the endings of every known breached hash beginning with those five characters, and your browser compares the rest of the hash locally.

Neither your password nor your full hash is sent, and we do not receive or store anything you enter. The request goes directly from your browser to Have I Been Pwned, which is subject to its own privacy policy. As with any web request, the service can see your IP address. The password and passphrase generators make no such request.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information to use the tools, and there is no sign-up. We do not sell personal data. We do not build profiles of individual visitors from what you type, because what you type does not reach us.

Two third-party services used on the site do process limited technical data, described below: Google Analytics, for aggregate traffic measurement, and Google AdSense, for advertising.

Analytics

Google Analytics helps us understand which pages are used and roughly where visitors come from, so we can tell whether a guide is helpful and whether the site works on different devices. It may process a truncated IP address, browser and device information, the pages viewed and the referring page, and it may set cookies. It does not receive the passwords you generate or type, and the site does not send custom events containing anything you enter.

Advertising and Google AdSense

KeyGuard Pro displays advertising through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site or to other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet. The ads are selected by Google, not by us, and they have no access to what you type into the tools.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. To understand how Google uses data from sites that use its services, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent and regional choices

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for their choices through Google's consent message, which appears on the site and is delivered by the Google services loaded here. Until a choice is made, advertising storage, ad user data, ad personalisation and analytics storage are set to "denied" for those regions. Visitors in some US states can manage their advertising privacy choices through Google's privacy message, which appears where state law requires it.

Cookies and local storage

We do not set first-party cookies, and the tools do not use local storage or session storage. The cookies that may be set are those placed by Google Analytics and Google AdSense. The Cookie Policy explains what they are used for and how to block or delete them in your browser.

How long data is kept

We keep no personal data ourselves, so there is no retention period on our side. Google's retention of analytics and advertising data is set by Google and described in its privacy documentation. Aggregate statistics that contain no personal data may be kept indefinitely.

Your choices and data deletion requests

Because the site has no accounts and stores nothing you enter, there is usually no personal record for us to delete. If you believe we hold something about you, email keyguard-pro@outlook.com with the subject "data request" and describe what you are looking for; we will confirm what exists and delete anything we control. Requests are answered within 30 days.

For data held by Google for analytics or advertising, use the controls Google provides: Ads Settings for advertising personalisation and your Google Account for activity data. You can also clear or block cookies in your browser at any time; the tools will continue to work.

Children's privacy

KeyGuard Pro is a general-audience tool. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Passwords are handled in the page and are not transmitted, which removes the risk of interception in transit. No system is perfectly secure, so we also recommend that you store generated passwords in a reputable password manager and turn on two-factor authentication for important accounts.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or the services it uses change. The date below shows the latest revision, and material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact
