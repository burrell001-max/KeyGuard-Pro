The strength score is an estimate, not a security guarantee

The score and crack time shown by the checker are estimates based on the length and character variety of the text you enter, with penalties for a small set of common patterns. The estimate cannot know whether a password has appeared in a data breach, whether it is derived from public information about you, or how a particular website stores and protects it. A high score does not mean an account cannot be compromised, and a low score does not mean an account has been compromised. The guide on password entropy explains the reasoning and its limits.
